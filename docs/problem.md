# Problem

AI skills are moving from private prompts into portable executable assets. That is useful, but it creates a governance gap.

A recipient needs more than a downloadable skill directory. They need portable evidence answering:

- what the skill declares it can do
- what authority it has for each action class
- who approved that authority
- what happened when the skill tried to act
- why authority was promoted, demoted, or revoked

Without this layer, skill exchange creates a supply-chain and runtime-authority problem: the asset may be portable, but its trust history is not.

This profile defines a small evidence layer for that missing boundary.
